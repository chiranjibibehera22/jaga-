🧳 Before You Visit

Simple information for devotees visiting Puri.

📅

Check the date

Besha dates often follow the Odia lunar calendar. The same tithi does not fall on the same Gregorian date every year.

🙏

Respect temple rules

Follow the current entry, photography and conduct rules communicated by temple authorities.

📱

Check latest updates

Special rituals can change the normal sequence and darshan availability. Confirm the latest temple notice before travelling. Darshan timings are subject to temple Niti and may change.
